Thai word list for Report Check's dictionary check.
- words_th.txt, thailand_provinces_th.csv and countries_th.txt from PyThaiNLP
  (https://github.com/PyThaiNLP/pythainlp, pythainlp/corpus), released under
  CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain) - see pythainlp/corpus/corpus_license.md
- plus environmental / biology / lab terms added for AER reports.
Multi-word entries (with spaces) are left out.
